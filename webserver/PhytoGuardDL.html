About this tool

What is PhytoGuardDL?

A unified web-based predictor for plant immunity, stress biology researchers, breeders and functional genomicists. Built on 369,639 orthology-expanded protein sequences across 13 plant species (covering biotic defense, abiotic stress, and dual crosstalk), evaluated under orthogroup-aware cross-validation, and validated against independent biotic/abiotic GEO transcriptome datasets, co-expression networks, and an expanded 150-gene qRT-PCR benchmark.

Multi-pooling ESM-2 embeddings (5,120-D)
Each protein is encoded by concatenating Mean, Max, Std, and Min pooling across 1,280-dimensional layers of Meta AI's ESM-2 (650M parameters), preserving domain-localized signatures (such as NB-ARC and Kinase domains) in long multi-domain proteins.
Multi-species orthology expansion (13 genomes)
5,606 curated plant stress and defense seeds across 226 species expanded via OrthoFinder to 369,639 sequences (123,213 positive : 246,426 negative, 1:2 ratio) spanning Arabidopsis, rice, maize, soybean, tomato, wheat, barley, Vitis, Musa, Manihot, Amborella, Selaginella and Physcomitrium — covering ~500 million years of plant evolution.
Deep Stacking Ensemble architecture
Combines PyTorch ResMLP GPU neural networks, XGBoost GPU classifier, HistGradientBoosting, ExtraTrees, and RandomForest models in a stacking meta-ensemble to achieve optimal cross-species precision and sensitivity.
Designed for translational use
Prioritize candidate defense genes from proteomes, transcriptomic experiments, GWAS hits or de novo assemblies before committing to wet-lab characterization. Sub-second per-sequence inference; full-proteome screening on commodity GPUs.

Methodology

Methodological strategy

PhytoGuardDL combines protein language models, multi-scale stress and defense domain features, and deep stacking ensembles for unified plant biotic defense and abiotic stress gene discovery.

01
Multi-Species Curated Datasets
5,606 experimentally reviewed stress and defense seeds across 226 plant species expanded to 123,213 positive orthologs (Biotic Defense, Abiotic Stress, and Dual Crosstalk) across 13 genomes via OrthoFinder, balanced 1:2 with 246,426 strict negative controls (369,639 total).
02
Multi-Scale Features & ESM-2 (650M)
Combines 496-D sequence representations (AAC, DPC, 52 stress/defense Pfam channels, and 24 biophysical descriptors) with multi-pooled ESM-2 (650M, 5,120-D) transformer embeddings benchmarked up to 97.03% accuracy (AUROC 0.9911, MCC 0.9335).
03
52 Stress & Defense Pfam Channels
Integrates 31 biotic defense channels (NBS-LRR, Kinase, WRKY, PR1–14, RBOH) and 21 abiotic stress channels (HSP20/70/90, HSF, LEA/Dehydrin, DREB/CBF, Aquaporin, SOS1/NHX, SOD/CAT/APX, SnRK2/PP2C, ERF-VII).
04
Deep Stacking Super-Ensemble
Blends probability outputs from Deep MLP neural networks, XGBoost, HistGradientBoosting, ExtraTrees, and RandomForest via a LogisticRegression meta-learner (95.23% accuracy, 0.9777 AUROC, 0.8919 MCC) with calibrated threshold 0.3731.
05
Multi-Tier Cross-Validation
Evaluated under Stratified 5-Fold CV (92.39% Acc, 0.9610 AUROC, 0.8268 MCC), 13-Species Leave-One-Species-Out (LOSO: 0.7990 AUROC), and strict 5-Fold Orthogroup GroupKFold across 24,663 orthogroups to quantify zero-homology generalization.
06
3-Tier Independent Biological Validation
Confirmed against independent NCBI GEO biotic/abiotic DEGs (up to 3.61× odds ratio, p = 3.20e-29), 156-sample transcriptional co-expression networks (max r = 0.771), and a 150-gene qRT-PCR benchmark (83.33% accuracy; 100% biotic / 98% abiotic sensitivity).
Computational & Biological Validation

Validated across six independent biological & mechanistic tiers

PhytoGuardDL is rigorously validated for Molecular Plant and Nucleic Acids Research publication standards — spanning six independent biotic and abiotic NCBI GEO transcriptomes, co-expression networks, a 150-gene experimental qRT-PCR benchmark, leak-free feature ablation & calibration, exact TreeSHAP + in silico alanine mutagenesis, and a 13-species evolutionary crop landscape.

Across 27,367 TAIR10 proteins tested on 4 AtGenExpress abiotic stresses and 2 pathogen infections, Abiotic-predicted genes are enriched in 4/4 abiotic stresses (OR 1.89–2.91×) with zero Biotic-axis leakage (0.09–1.24×), Dual Crosstalk hubs enrich in both, and Basal controls are depleted (0.20–0.62×) in 6/6 systems.

Quick start guide

PhytoGuardDL is a two-phase deep learning and machine learning platform predicting plant biotic defense and abiotic stress response genes and decoding their functional biology. Get started in under 2 minutes:

1

Get your protein sequence

Retrieve full-length canonical protein sequences from UniProtKB, NCBI Protein, or Phytozome. Full-length sequences ensure complete domain and biophysical signature extraction.

2

Paste or upload FASTA file

Use the Single Sequence, Batch (FASTA), or Upload File tab. Enter an optional email for notification, or leave it blank for instant on-screen results.

3

Run Two-Phase Stress & Defense Analysis

Click "Run prediction". Phase I scores Stress & Defense probability across 5 DL/ML models (threshold 0.3731). Phase II decodes Biological Axis (Biotic Defense, Abiotic Stress, or Dual Crosstalk), verified Pfam/GO/KEGG annotations, locus domain coordinates, Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy, and 20-AA biophysics.

4

Explore interactive tabs & export 600 DPI figures

Hover over any chart bar or hydropathy point for exact metrics, click domains to highlight residues in the sequence viewer, export 4 publication-grade 600 DPI figures (PNG, JPG, TIFF, SVG), or download complete 31-column CSV, TSV, FASTA, and JSON files.

Interactive Sample Output (NAR Tutorial): Launch live interactive predictions identically to real user submissions.

Input formats

FASTA format (recommended):

>FLS2_AT5G46330
MGDDEAPGKGSFVCDEHGSGKTIGAGFAGDDAPRAVFPSIV
GRPRHQGVMVGMGQKDSYVGDEAQSKRGILTLKYPIEHGI

Raw sequence:

MGDDEAPGKGSFVCDEHGSGKTIGAGFAGDDAPRAVFPSIVGRPRHQGVMVGMGQKDS
Important: Always use full-length canonical protein sequences (DNA/CDS inputs are automatically rejected). The minimum sequence length is 30 amino acids.

Ambiguous amino acids are automatically mapped: J→L, B→N, Z→Q, O→K, U→C, X→A. Stop codons (*) and gaps (−) are automatically cleaned.

Recommended sequence sources:

  • UniProtKB — reviewed (Swiss-Prot) Viridiplantae entries preferred
  • NCBI Protein — RefSeq NP_ / XP_ plant accessions
  • InterPro / Pfam — protein domain families and signatures

Understanding results & the 4-Tab Workbench

Phase I Unified Stress & Defense Screening:

  • Ensemble Score (0.0 – 1.0): Stacked meta-probability combining Deep MLP (DL), XGBoost, HistGBM, ExtraTrees, and Random Forest via LogisticRegression. The calibrated production cutoff is 0.3731.
  • Confidence Levels: HIGH (|score − 0.3731| > 0.30), MEDIUM (|score − 0.3731| > 0.10), or MODERATE.
  • Interactive Cutoff Explorer: Drag the threshold slider (0.10 – 0.90) in Tab 1 to test sensitivity/specificity trade-offs live.

Phase II Functional Genomics Workbench (4 Interactive Tabs):

  • Tab 1 — Overview & Model Consensus: Interactive 5-model probability bar chart with hover tooltips, 3-axis probabilities (Biotic Defense, Abiotic Stress, Dual Crosstalk), functional class, localization, signal peptide/TM summary, hormonal pathway, clickable QuickGO, KEGG Plant Pathway, and InterPro Pfam chips, plus recommended wet-lab/breeding validation assay.
  • Tab 2 — Interactive Sequence & Domains: Linear domain architecture track with hover tooltips and 1-click residue highlighting in the formatted sequence viewer, plus an interactive 19-aa sliding-window Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy curve with +1.6 TM helix cutoff.
  • Tab 3 — Biophysics & Genomics Links: 8 exact physicochemical descriptors (MW, pI, GRAVY, net charge, aliphatic index, aromaticity, cysteine %, length), interactive 20-AA composition chart, and verified external cross-links (NCBI BLASTp POST, InterProScan, UniProtKB, AlphaFold DB, PlantReactome, NCBI Gene, and TAIR for AGI loci).
  • Tab 4 — Export Data & 600 DPI Figures: Download 31-column CSV/TSV, positive FASTA, full JSON, or open the 600 DPI Publication Figure Studio (Figure 1: Model Consensus & Axis, Figure 2: Domain Map, Figure 3: Hydropathy & TM Topology, Figure 4: 20-AA Biophysics & Validation Priority).

Batch prediction & Job Bookmarking

Batch paste supports up to 1,000 sequences; the Upload File tab accepts up to 50,000 FASTA sequences for whole-proteome screening.

Bookmarkable Job Link: Every batch submission generates a unique Job ID (e.g. pg_job_xxxx). Bookmark the permanent URL shown in the results banner to reload your results at any time.

Candidate-Only Display Filtering: The on-screen list shows the top 50 predicted stress and defense candidates sorted by ensemble score; clicking any row opens the full 4-tab interactive modal. The complete dataset (all positive and basal sequences) is included in all downloads.

Email is 100% Optional: Leave the email field blank for instant on-screen results and a bookmarkable link.

Download options

PhytoGuardDL exports clean, publication-ready 31-column tables with all Phase I and Phase II annotations:

  • CSV / TSV (31 columns) — name, length, prediction, biological_axis, confidence, ensemble_score, threshold, all 5 base model scores (mlp, xgboost, hgb, extratrees, rf), 3-axis probabilities (biotic, abiotic, crosstalk), phase2_functional_class, subcellular_localization, signal_peptide, tm_helices, phytohormone_pathway, locus_domain_coordinates, go_terms, kegg_pathways, pfam_domains, recommended_validation_assay, and 6 biophysical properties (MW, pI, GRAVY, charge, aliphatic index, cysteine %).
  • FASTA (Stress/Defense Positive) — Positive-class sequences with ensemble score, biological axis, and verdict in the FASTA header.
  • JSON — Complete structured API payload including hydropathy profile and 20-AA composition.
  • 600 DPI Publication Figures — PNG, JPG, TIFF, and SVG exports for Figures 1–4.

REST API & Web Services

PhytoGuardDL provides an open-access JSON REST API at https://phytoguarddl.cdfd.org.in (no authentication or registration required). Download the ready-to-run Python template client: phytoguarddl_client.py ↓

Single sequence endpoint:

curl -X POST https://phytoguarddl.cdfd.org.in/api/predict \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{"sequence": "MGDDEAPGKGSF...", "name": "FLS2_AT5G46330"}'

Batch FASTA endpoint:

curl -X POST https://phytoguarddl.cdfd.org.in/api/batch \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{"fasta": ">FLS2_AT5G46330\nMKLLSKT...\n>DREB1A_AT4G25480\nMNSFS..."}'

Job retrieval & health endpoints: GET /api/job/{job_id} · GET /health · GET /download/python-client

Standalone & Docker Container Installation

Run PhytoGuardDL on any operating system via Docker/Podman (no manual software installation required) or natively with Python 3.9+.

# Option 1: Docker / Podman container (cross-platform)
git clone https://github.com/lgi-cdfd/PhytoGuardDL
cd PhytoGuardDL
docker build -t phytoguarddl .
docker run -p 8000:8000 phytoguarddl

# Option 2: Native Python
pip install -r requirements.txt
python webserver/server_phytoguarddl.py --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000

Frequently asked questions

Q: Is registration or email required to use PhytoGuardDL?
A: No. PhytoGuardDL is a free, open-access web server compliant with Nucleic Acids Research (NAR) guidelines. No account or email is required.

Q: Does PhytoGuardDL predict both biotic defense and abiotic stress genes?
A: Yes. Trained on 369,639 sequences expanded from 5,606 curated seeds across 226 plant species, PhytoGuardDL classifies both biotic defense (PTI/ETI receptors, kinases, WRKY, PR proteins) and abiotic stress (drought, salt, heat, cold, oxidative, hypoxia) as well as Dual Crosstalk hubs.

Q: What species does PhytoGuardDL support?
A: Validated across 13 plant species (monocots, dicots, gymnosperms, and bryophytes) with 95.23% 6-model ensemble accuracy (0.9777 AUROC, 0.8919 MCC), 92.39% Stratified 5-Fold CV accuracy, and 0.7990 13-species LOSO AUROC. Supports all land plant proteomes.
